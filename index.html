The Original Production

The Wrong Script is a short interactive film about a production that has lost its script. You arrive on set, choose a door, and try to keep the thing running. It has been here from the beginning, and it is still here now — with a Lobby built around it rather than on top of it.

  • Three routes through the building, plus a fourth door that only opens once all three script fragments are found.
  • Three endings, a post-credits scene, and a replay that starts clean.
  • About fifteen minutes for a single route, longer if you keep poking at the props.
  • No download, no account, no install. It runs in your browser and saves your place there.
