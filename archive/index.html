The Archive is the universe keeping its own records: the experiences that have a real ending, the threads that run between different rooms, and the details that are easy to walk past. Nothing here is a progress bar, and nothing here is counting you.

Completed Files

Three experiences with a real ending

These are filed here because they finish. Each one has a conclusion the work actually reaches, rather than a score that keeps climbing. Open any of them and you start at the beginning, because the Archive does not know what you have already seen.

Collections

A collection is an argument, not a shelf. Each one gathers work from different rooms around a single idea, and every entry opens the thing itself so the argument can be tested.
