2. WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A read-only Creator Studio at /creator-studio/, in the established architecture:
a document shell with the shared platform navigation, a content data file, a
renderer that builds nodes with createElement/textContent, and a dedicated
stylesheet. No framework, no dependency, no backend, no storage.

The page opens with a status line that states its limits plainly:

    read-only - no accounts - no uploads - submissions not open

2.1 The Desk
Three paragraphs and one pull quote introducing the Studio as the creative
space of the universe, and stating the central idea: people should eventually
have opportunities to help create the world they explore, without implying that
those opportunities exist yet.

2.2 The Format
Five creative formats, each with a number, a title, a lede and a short list of
creative expectations (20 expectations in total):

  01  Stories and Writing
      Original stories, fiction and creative writing, interactive and branching
      narratives, character and world-building.
  02  Games and Interactive Experiences
      Original games, puzzles and challenges, interactive experiments,
      collaborative experiences.
  03  Visual Art and Design
      Illustration, digital artwork, photography and visual storytelling,
      graphic and creative design.
  04  Music and Sound
      Original composition, instrumental work, original sound design, other
      creative audio projects.
  05  Experiences and Collaborations
      Creative events, group projects, community challenges, experimental
      formats that do not fit the categories above.

No technical submission requirement is invented anywhere in this section.

2.3 The Standard
Seven editorial principles, each with a stable id:

  originality        Your own work, and respect for everyone else's
  teen-audience      Written for a teenage audience
  romance            Age-appropriate romantic themes, under review
  harassment         No harassment, bullying or targeted abuse
  impersonation      No impersonation or deceptive representation
  privacy            Privacy, consent and identifying details
  review             Reviewed before anything is published

The section states that acceptance is not automatic and that not every
submission will be published.

2.4 The Review Process
The five intended stages, in order:

    Draft -> Submitted -> Under review -> Changes requested -> Published

plus the two other outcomes, Rejected and Unpublished, and three notes covering
ownership of the work, how long review takes, and the scope of what is reviewed.
The section explains that contributors keep control of their original work, that
changes may be requested, that approval precedes publication, and that published
material may be removed to protect the community or uphold the standards. No
submission or moderation system was built.

2.5 Not Yet
Five clearly stated absences: creator accounts, uploads, submissions,
collaborative creation tools, and any publication date. The wording is
deliberate rather than apologetic, and no date is promised.

2.6 Structure of the page
Sections carry real ids (#desk, #format, #standard, #review, #not-yet) with a
jump list of real anchors, so the page is deep-linkable. An unknown fragment
leaves the page intact and does not throw.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - No accounts, login, profiles or authentication of any kind.
  - No upload control, no submission form, no input element at all. The only
    control on the page is the existing small-screen navigation toggle.
  - No moderation dashboard, review queue or publishing path.
  - No comments, messaging, reactions, votes, rankings or counters.
  - No new localStorage, sessionStorage, cookie, IndexedDB or network call.
  - No analytics, tracking or third-party service.
  - No dependency, package manifest, framework, bundler or build step.
  - No fake buttons, disabled controls that look active, or success messages for
    work that is not submitted or stored.
  - No decorative element that implies a feature that does not exist.

The page therefore says what the Studio will be, and shows that none of it is
open yet, without simulating any of it.

6.6 Historical failure, recorded in full

  At revision 17 the new static suite failed with three errors. All three were
  defects in the verifier I had just written, not in the application:

    FAIL  the standard covers the required ground
          The check looked for the word "originality" in the Standard, and the
          first principle expressed the idea without using that word.
          Resolution: the principle now reads "Originality is the baseline. A
          piece must be yours, or used with permission you can show...". The
          assertion was left strict and unweakened; the content was made to say
          plainly what the brief requires it to say.

    FAIL  the Social Room lists the Studio as open with a working link
          The check read window.SOCIAL_ROOM from the Creator Studio's own
          sandbox, where that object does not exist.
          Resolution: the check now loads assets/js/social-room-content.js in its
          own vm context before reading the object. Application code untouched.

    FAIL  the renderer builds nodes without innerHTML
          The string "innerHTML" appeared in a comment in the renderer explaining
          that innerHTML is not used.
          Resolution: the check strips comments before scanning, so the
          assertion stays strict while comments cannot satisfy or trip it.

  After those three corrections the suite reported all 30 checks passed, and it
  has passed unchanged since.
