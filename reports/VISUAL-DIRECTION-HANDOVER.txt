1. WHAT WAS BUILT, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every existing room now carries a full-viewport, silent video atmosphere behind
its content, with a poster frame underneath it, a gradient scrim and a
per-room colour tint over it, and one shared floating pill in the bottom corner
that reports the room and its playback state and expands into master audio, a
music level, an effects level, an ambience level and an independent background
video switch. Nothing was rebuilt, no room was added, no story, puzzle, ending,
save key or navigation destination changed, and "/film/" was not touched.

5. HOW IT WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 The layer

  .ambience            position:fixed; inset:0; z-index:-1;
                       pointer-events:none
  .ambience__video     object-fit:cover; the poster is painted underneath, so a
                       visitor sees a still frame while the video loads and
                       keeps seeing it if the video never arrives
  .ambience__tint      per-room colour wash
  .ambience__scrim     gradient from the page colour at the bottom, for text

Because the layer is pointer-events:none and sits behind everything, it cannot
intercept a click, a drag or a focus. It cannot interfere with gameplay.

body[data-room] has its background-color set to transparent so the wash is
visible at all; every card, panel, bar and button keeps its own opaque
background, so surfaces that already carried text are unchanged. Text sitting
directly on the page now falls on the scrim.

5.2 Motion rules, as implemented

  - The video element is created with muted = true, playsInline = true and
    loop = true, and no autoplay attribute is written anywhere. Playback is
    started by script only.
  - A muted autoplay attempt is made once, because browsers permit muted
    autoplay. If the browser refuses, the layer stays on its poster and the
    pill says so ("atmosphere blocked") rather than pretending to play.
  - prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is read with matchMedia. When it matches, no
    video is fetched and no autoplay is attempted; the poster alone is shown.
  - visibilitychange pauses the video; pagehide pauses it and drops the source,
    so leaving the room stops the transfer and nothing keeps playing in a
    background tab.
  - One layer, one video per page. tools/verify-ambience.js asserts a single
    insertBefore and a single createElement for the video, so two decorative
    videos cannot accumulate on one page.

5.3 The controller

  Compact state   room name and playback state ("Atmosphere - playing", or
                  "paused", "off" or "atmosphere blocked"), plus a small
                  waveform mark. The whole pill is one button.

  Expanded state  a panel with:
                    Master audio      a switch (mute everything, restore after)
                    Music             slider, or "-" when the room has none
                    Effects           slider, wired to the Arcade click channel
                    Ambience          slider
                    Background video  a switch, independent of all audio

  Behaviour       the sliders read the real element volumes when the panel
                  opens, so they never show an invented position. A slider for a
                  channel the room does not have is disabled and says so
                  ("Music is not present in this room") rather than sitting at a
                  fake 60%. The master switch only mutes and unmutes; it never
                  starts audio the room was not already playing.

  Keyboard        the compact pill is a button; the panel is a disclosure with
                  a labelled close control; Escape closes it and returns focus
                  to the pill. Rows are switches with role="switch" and
                  aria-checked, so their state is announced.

  Position        bottom-right on desktop, full width above the safe-area inset
                  on narrow screens, so it never sits under a thumb or off the
                  edge.

  Storage         none. The pill remembers nothing between visits, which is why
                  no key was added. It is a control, not a preference store.

7. ONE EXISTING ASSERTION WAS WIDENED - PLEASE REVIEW THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

tools/verify-creator-studio.js previously asserted that the Studio page loads
exactly four assets ("expected 2 stylesheets and 2 scripts"). That count was a
proxy for its real intent: nothing remote, nothing unexpected, nothing missing.

The shared shell legitimately adds three assets to that page, so the count
broke. The check now uses an explicit allowlist instead:

  allowed = /^(?:\.\.\/)?assets\/(?:css|js)\/(?:creator-studio|lobby|ambience|media-controller)[a-z-]*\.(?:css|js)$/

and still asserts, for every reference, that it is not remote, that it matches
the allowlist, and that the file exists on disk.

What this keeps: remote assets, third-party assets, unexpected local assets and
missing files all still fail.
What it loses: the exact "four" figure. That figure was the weakest part of the
check and is now replaced by a stronger one.

This is the only existing assertion changed anywhere in this pass, and it is
called out here rather than buried, because widening a test to make a change
pass is exactly the sort of thing that should be visible.

9. LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1.  THREE VIDEOS, ELEVEN ROOMS. Only abstract-motion, lighthouse and library
      exist. Rooms share footage and are distinguished by tint and scrim. The
      brief's per-room atmosphere is met by colour and grading, not by unique
      footage. Supplying more video would allow genuinely distinct rooms.

  2.  THE ASSETS ARE NOT RIGHTS-CLEARED, AND I CANNOT CLAIM THEY ARE. The brief
      asks for "rights-cleared" video. assets/media/ASSET-CREDITS.txt records
      all six assets as owner-supplied with source, creator, licence and
      attribution unresolved. Nothing in this pass establishes a licence, and
      this report does not describe any asset as cleared, licensed or safe to
      publish. If the project is published publicly, that question needs an
      owner decision first.

  3.  TEXT CONTRAST OVER MOVING VIDEO WAS NOT MEASURED. The scrim is designed
      for contrast and the earlier Phase 8 flat-background measurements still
      apply to the surfaces themselves, but automated contrast measurement
      cannot evaluate text over a moving image. It needs a visual review on
      real screens. This is CODE REVIEW ONLY, not a measurement.

  4.  REDUCED MOTION AND THE PAUSE RULES ARE CODE REVIEW ONLY in this pass.
      They are implemented and statically asserted, but this pass did not
      emulate prefers-reduced-motion in a browser, and did not simulate
      visibilitychange. The behaviour is asserted in source, not observed at
      runtime.

  5.  CHROMIUM ONLY. Firefox, WebKit and Edge cannot launch in this sandbox
      (missing host libraries, no root, no package sources), as recorded in the
      Phase 8 handover. Mobile figures are 390x844 viewport emulation with
      touch enabled, not a physical phone.

  6.  NO ASSISTIVE-TECHNOLOGY TESTING. The switches expose role="switch" and
      aria-checked and the panel is a labelled disclosure, but no screen reader
      was run. Nothing here is an accessibility certification.

  7.  THE SLIDERS ARE NOT PERSISTED, by design. A visitor who mutes the
      atmosphere is not remembered on the next visit. This is deliberate - it
      keeps the pass free of new storage - but it is a real behaviour a visitor
      may notice.

  8.  THE PILL OVERLAPS CONTENT on short pages, because it is fixed. It is
      offset above the safe-area inset and never covers a navigation control,
      but on a very short viewport it sits over the footer area.

  9.  EXISTING PHASE 8 DEFECTS REMAIN OPEN. DEF-01 (13px Lobby card meta links)
      and DEF-03 (one 41px control on four rooms) were not addressed here; this
      pass did not touch their styling. DEF-02 stays protected with "/film/".
