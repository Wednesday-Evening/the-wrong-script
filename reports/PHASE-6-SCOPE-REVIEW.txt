3. FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

F1 - "Revisit completed experiences" conflicts with the no-save-reading rule.
  The purpose statement and area A imply the Archive knows what the visitor has
  finished. It cannot: that requires reading the three save keys (tws,
  wrong-script.story.the-ninth-bell.v1,
  wrong-script.arcade.signal-path.v1), which sections 3 and 6 of the brief
  forbid.

  Recommendation: the Archive is a catalogue of works that HAVE conclusions, not
  a record of what the visitor completed. State that in The Record and in a
  one-line note beside Completed Files. Keep "your progress", "you have
  completed" and "continue where you left off" out of Archive copy.

  If per-visitor completion is genuinely wanted, it is a separate, explicitly
  authorised decision requiring read-only save access, and the Archive must
  still never write. See D1.

F2 - The existing Lobby card promises content the project does not hold.
  index.html:132 currently describes the Archive as "drafts, deleted scenes, and
  what this film used to be before it lost the script". No draft artefacts,
  deleted scenes or earlier film versions exist anywhere in the repository, and
  the proposed scope does not produce them either.

  Recommendation: Phase 6 must rewrite that card copy to describe what is
  actually delivered. Left as is, it would be the one misleading promise on the
  page, and the project's own test conventions already assert that stale
  "coming soon" copy is removed.

F3 - Completed Files should list three works, not two.
  The Ninth Bell (five endings) and the original film (three endings plus
  post-credits) are correct. SIGNAL PATH also has a genuine, finite conclusion:
  six levels, a final completion state and a replay control. Excluding it would
  make the Archive silently ignore the Arcade.

  Recommendation: include SIGNAL PATH, labelled as a game with a definite end.
  Exclude the Gallery (a collection, no conclusion) and the Social Room
  (read-only boards, no ending). See D2.

F4 - Discoveries are real, but they are corners, not buried secrets.
  Verifiable candidates, each confirmed in source:
    1. The film has three distinct endings, not one.
    2. A post-credits scene follows the "leave the production" ending.
    3. The film's crew lounge only completes when all four crew have been met.
    4. The art studio references Room 13, and the painting chosen there
       reappears at the final cut.
    5. Any film scene can be linked directly by its hash and restores exactly.
    6. The Ninth Bell has five endings; the hopeful one needs two separate
       discoveries.
    7. The Ninth Bell can be ended early, deliberately, and still reach a
       complete ending.
    8. SIGNAL PATH's beacon refuses to open while fragments remain, and says so.
    9. The Gallery admits that its photography exhibit used no camera, and that
       its music exhibit is a written cue rather than a recording.
   10. Social Room entries double as deep links.

  Recommendation: publish about eight, not all ten, and frame the section as
  optional corners and details rather than promising hidden secrets. Item 6 is a
  real spoiler and must sit behind disclosure. Item 10 is a navigation quirk
  rather than a discovery - drop it, or mention it in The Record.

F5 - The navigation edit surface is larger than the brief implies, and precise.
  Making the Archive live requires touching 7 HTML files (the nav placeholder),
  the Lobby card, and two paragraphs that currently say the Archive is still
  being built (gallery/index.html:100, social-room/index.html:70), plus the
  README line listing the Archive as reserved (README.md:76). The Lobby hero
  eyebrow currently reads "Phase five - the social room is open" and needs
  Phase 6 copy.

  The film is excluded from this list by design: it has no platform navigation.

  Recommendation: treat those edits as part of Phase 6 scope, and add a static
  assertion that no page still labels the Archive "Soon" or "still being built"
  - the pattern tools/verify-social-room.js already uses at line 351.

F6 - No filters, no search, no new storage.
  Three completed works, three collections and roughly eight discoveries do not
  justify filters. In-page section navigation with real anchors is enough, and it
  keeps every section deep-linkable without storing anything.

  Recommendation: sections and anchors only. No localStorage, no sessionStorage,
  no new key, no filter state. A static test should prove the Archive scripts
  contain no storage API reference and none of the three existing keys.

F7 - Deep-link contracts already exist; the Archive must join them.
  Verified working patterns: /gallery/#<exhibit-id>, /social-room/#<entry-id>,
  and film scenes by hash. Both Gallery and Social Room handle an unknown hash
  with an explanatory note rather than a broken view.

  Recommendation: the Archive must do the same for /archive/#<unknown>, and
  every Archive link must use the real ids listed in section 2 rather than
  approximations. A static test should resolve every href in the Archive data
  against actual exhibit, entry, route and scene ids.

5. RISKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R1  Spoiler leakage in preview text or aria labels.
    Mitigation: generic summary text; the revealing sentence lives only inside
    the disclosure.
R2  Wrong or dead anchors.
    Mitigation: static test resolves every href against real ids and routes.
R3  Duplicating story or gallery prose.
    Mitigation: one or two sentences per entry, then a link. No scene text.
R4  Accidental personalisation language.
    Mitigation: copy review pass; banned phrases listed in F1.
R5  Navigation inconsistency across seven pages.
    Mitigation: one assertion that every page's Archive link resolves relative
    to its own depth and carries no "Soon" badge.
R6  Implying member activity in The Record.
    Mitigation: selection principles only; no attendance, awards or counts.
R7  Scope creep into The Creator Studio, which also carries a "Soon" badge.
    Mitigation: leave it untouched; the brief does not authorise it.

8. DECISIONS NEEDED BEFORE THE PHASE 6 PROMPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  Should the Archive show what the visitor personally completed?
    Recommendation: NO. Keep it a catalogue. Personal completion needs read-only
    access to the three save keys, which the brief forbids and which would couple
    a browse-only page to saved progress.

D2  Should SIGNAL PATH appear under Completed Files?
    Recommendation: YES. It has a genuine finite ending and a replay control.

D3  How should spoilers be revealed?
    Recommendation: native <details>/<summary>, labelled "Spoiler". Keyboard and
    screen-reader behaviour come for free, with no JavaScript and no motion.

D4  Should the Archive carry a closing "alongside the other rooms" paragraph, as
    the Gallery and Social Room do?
    Recommendation: YES, for consistency - and it is the natural place to state
    the read-only, no-personal-data boundary.

If the owner is content with the recommended defaults, D1-D4 are sufficient to
write the implementation prompt without further clarification.
