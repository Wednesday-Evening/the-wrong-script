================================================================================
2. WHAT WAS DELIVERED
================================================================================

An exhibition at /gallery/, reachable from the Lobby, that belongs to the
universe without copying the film, the Stories or the Arcade.

The visitor arrives at a title wall, then a single continuous hanging wall of
eight exhibits grouped by six shelves: Visual Art, Photography, Poetry,
Writing, Illustration and Original Music. Category filters sit above the wall
and announce their state. Every exhibit opens in a detail panel that carries
the work itself - prose, poem, score sketch or artwork - with its category,
medium, collection, description, studio attribution and an honesty note
saying what the piece is and is not. Each exhibit is addressable
(/gallery/#exhibit-id), so a link can be shared or reloaded, and an unknown
link is explained rather than silently ignored.

The Gallery is browse-only. There is no account, no upload, no submission, no
like, no comment, no counter, no ranking and no personal data. Nothing plays
sound, nothing autoplays, and the page stores nothing in the browser.

Editorial identity: the near-black surfaces, deep red accents and muted gold
rules of the existing universe, carried by a serif display face for titles and
wide, generous spacing for the wall. Motion is a short fade and lift on the
frames, and it is switched off entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.

================================================================================
6. CONTENT MODEL AND FUTURE COMPATIBILITY
================================================================================

Data and presentation are separated. gallery-exhibits.js declares plain data;
gallery.js reads it, validates it, and draws. Adding an exhibit means adding
one object to the collection and, if it has artwork, one local file.

The shape already accommodates a future member exhibit (id, title, category,
body or media, credit), but nothing in this phase reads, writes or publishes
submitted content. There is no upload control, no submission route, no draft
state and no moderation surface. The platform rule that member work must be
reviewed before publication is therefore intact and unexercised: no route or
control exists that could bypass it, and the renderer only ever draws fields
as text and an image src from the local collection - it never injects HTML,
executes content, or fetches anything.

The renderer validates defensively: unknown categories, missing ids, duplicate
ids, missing titles and missing bodies are dropped rather than rendered, and an
empty result shows the empty state with a way back to everything.

9. ACCESSIBILITY, RESPONSIVE AND SAFETY FINDINGS
================================================================================

Accessibility
- Landmarks and headings: one h1 per page, sections in order, the wall is a
  list of articles with headings.
- Skip link to the wall.
- Filters are buttons with aria-pressed, not styled divs, and they carry their
  exhibit count in the accessible name.
- The status line is aria-live="polite", so filtering is announced.
- Cards are links (#exhibit-id), so the panel is reachable, shareable and
  works with the browser's Back button.
- The panel is a labelled region; opening it moves focus to its heading, and
  closing it (button or Escape) returns focus to the card that opened it.
- Artwork carries written alt text describing the scene; the figure is omitted
  entirely for text-only exhibits.
- Visible focus rings are defined for every interactive control, and the
  browser run confirmed a ring at all fourteen Tab stops.
- Colour is never the only signal: categories are written, filters state their
  state, and every card names its shelf.

Responsive
- Mobile-first CSS; the wall is a single column on a phone and a three-column
  grid on desktop, using minmax(min(100%, 262px), 1fr) so a frame can never
  force a horizontal scrollbar.
- Measured: no horizontal overflow on any of the seven routes at 390px or
  1280px, including with the exhibit panel open on a phone.
- Body copy stays at a comfortable measure; touch targets measured at 44px and
  48px.

Motion and sound
- Transitions and the frame reveal are removed under prefers-reduced-motion
  (verified by emulation: 0ms transition, 0ms animation, immediate scrolling).
- No sound anywhere in the Gallery, nothing autoplays, no flashing or strobing,
  no timers, no streaks, no countdown pressure.

Safety and privacy
- No personal information is requested, collected, displayed or implied. There
  are no member names, no visitor counts, no ratings, no comments and no
  popularity signals anywhere on the page.
- Copy is suitable for a broad teen audience: the works are atmospheric and
  restrained, with no violence, no sexual content and no dangerous
  instruction.
- All content and artwork is original to this project and credited to The
  Wrong Script Studio.

10. KNOWN LIMITATIONS AND UNVERIFIED ITEMS
================================================================================

1. The web route-smoke check call-6328b34d remains a recorded failure, and it
   was not re-run under its original definition. Its expectations were wrong
   for these pages (title case against CSS-uppercased headings, and an h1 that
   film/index.html does not have), so the failure belongs to the check, not to
   the pages. It is not retried with corrected strings because the checker
   treats changed expectations as a changed test definition. Revision 19 does
   not change this: the seven-route, two-viewport coverage it repeats comes
   from the browser check call-04972ce8 and its re-run, which is a different
   check. The original failure, its cause and its resolution are kept in
   section 8 rather than deleted. If the project wants a clean preset record,
   the check should be redefined deliberately rather than retried.

2. The empty state is CODE REVIEW ONLY. It is defensive, and the shipped
   collection cannot reach it. It was not forced in a browser.

3. Reduced motion was verified with browser emulation, not on a real device
   with the OS setting enabled.

4. Keyboard verification was real browser Tab traversal with computed-style
   focus-ring checks. No screen reader was run, and no assistive-technology
   certification is claimed.

5. No search is implemented, by design. The prompt made search optional; the
   six shelves are a small enough set to browse directly, and a search box that
   filtered eight items would add a control without adding discovery. The
   unmatched-state requirement is satisfied by the unknown-exhibit note.

6. Visual design was not reviewed by a human. The layout, contrast and
   composition were built to the stated direction and checked for overflow and
   target size, but "looks right" is a judgement this report cannot make.

7. The artwork is original abstract SVG drawn in code. It is deliberate and
   consistent, but it is illustration of a mood, not photography; the
   Photography shelf is a drawn still rather than a captured frame, and the
   exhibit copy is written so that nothing overclaims.

8. The three text-only works have no media file. That is intentional and
   stated in the panel, not a missing asset.

9. Everything remains single-browser and static. Nothing syncs across devices,
   and the report makes no claim that it does.

10. The dependency scan is not_applicable, not clean (section 8B). With no
   manifest and no dependencies there was nothing to resolve, which is the
   intended state of this project, but it means the scan offers no evidence
   about the source, the runtime or the hosting environment.

11. SHARED FILE CHANGES AND RATIONALE
================================================================================

Five HTML files outside gallery/ changed, and each change is a navigation
entry: one link to the Gallery in the shared header of stories/index.html,
arcade/index.html, stories/the-ninth-bell/index.html and
arcade/signal-path/index.html, plus the Lobby's Gallery card becoming a real
link in index.html.

Rationale: the universe is navigated by a repeated header. Adding the Gallery
only to the Lobby would have made it reachable from exactly one place and left
visitors stranded in another destination with no way across. The alternative -
per-page bespoke links - would have been more code, not less. No CSS, script,
story data or game logic was modified in those files.

assets/css/lobby.css, assets/js/lobby.js, assets/js/story-engine.js,
assets/js/arcade*.js, assets/js/arcade-levels.js and every file under film/
were not modified.

New development scripts (tools/verify-gallery.js, tools/verify-gallery-ui.cjs)
are checks, not site code. The site itself still ships no dependency, no
manifest and no build step; the browser check finds Playwright in the
environment and says so plainly if it is missing.
