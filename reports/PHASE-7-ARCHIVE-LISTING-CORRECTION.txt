3.2 WHY "Open now"

The convention was taken from the sibling Gallery listing in the same file
(lines 205-212), which is the established way this room marks a destination that
genuinely exists:

          link: { label: 'Walk the wall', href: '../gallery/' }

The Archive has been open since Phase 6, so it now uses the same status and the
same link shape. The link label follows the local idiom ("Walk the wall") with
"Open the record", and points at the real route ../archive/, which resolves from
social-room/ to archive/index.html.

3.3 WHY THE OLD TEXT WAS WRONG

The entry promised "drafts, deleted scenes, and what this film used to be" and
said the Archive was "Not built". Three separate inaccuracies:

  - The Archive is built: it opened in Phase 6 at /archive/.
  - It contains no drafts, deleted scenes or earlier film versions. Phase 6
    deliberately removed exactly that promise from the Lobby card, because those
    materials do not exist. This Social Room listing was the last surviving
    copy of the claim.
  - "Not built, and no date is being promised" is an accurate sentence about
    the Creator Studio, not about the Archive.

The replacement describes only what the Archive actually holds, verified against
the catalogue itself: three completed works (The Ninth Bell, The Original
Production, SIGNAL PATH), three curated collections, eight discoveries, and the
read-only catalogue framing. No new feature is claimed, and nothing is said
about drafts, previous versions, personal completion records or member activity.
