6. BACKGROUND-VIDEO BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Decorative video autoplays muted only; never with sound.
  - One decorative background video per room, enforced by the layer itself.
  - The video pauses when the page becomes hidden and when the visitor leaves
    the room; it does not keep playing in a background tab.
  - Reduced motion: the room goes still, with the poster shown instead of a
    moving wash (verified: status "still", paused true, transitions 0s).
  - Poster fallback: every video has a local poster, so a blocked or failed
    playback degrades to a still background rather than an empty panel.
  - The controller's video switch stops and restarts the background video
    (verified: paused true / status "off", then paused false / status "playing").
  - Story cinematics and gameplay sequences stay tied to their own events; this
    layer does not drive them.

7. CONTROLLER FUNCTIONALITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Collapsed pill:
  - Current room name, playback state in words, animated audio indicator while
    audio runs, mute badge, and the expander.
  - On a first visit it also carries the one sound question, in place.

Expanded panel:
  - Master audio switch, which overrides every channel.
  - Three independent sliders: music, effects, ambience.
  - Independent background-video switch.
  - Explicit states: playing, paused, muted, blocked, off, unavailable.
  - A recovery control when the browser has blocked playback, so audio can be
    started deliberately rather than silently failing.
  - A channel a room does not carry is marked absent and its slider is disabled,
    rather than pretending to control something.

Behaviour verified in a browser:
  - Escape collapses the panel and returns focus to the pill.
  - The panel reopens from the pill.
  - The ambience slider moves 60 to 20 and the value persists.
  - The video switch stops and restarts the background video.
  - The panel and the pill never cover the footer's last line: at the foot of
    the page the footer ends above the pill (footerBottom 708, mixerTop 739,
    92px of reserved padding).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. AUDIO IMPLEMENTATION AND ACTIVATION POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - All sound is generated in the browser with the Web Audio API by
    assets/js/audio-engine.js. No audio file was added in this pass.
  - Channels: music, effects, ambience. Each room declares which it carries and
    how its atmosphere is built (filter type, cutoff, gain, drift).
  - Interface cues are short and belong to the effects channel; arcade and
    gallery interactions route through the same engine, so there is one mixer
    and one policy rather than three competing ones.
  - Activation: nothing plays before the visitor answers the first-visit
    question. "Enter with sound" starts audio; "Continue silently" leaves it
    off. The answer is stored, so returning visitors are not asked again.
  - Master mute overrides every category. Category volumes are independent.
  - Leaving a room stops its audio; the engine exposes stop/stopBed/stopMusic so
    a room cannot leave a stream running behind the next one.
  - Malformed or missing preferences fall back to safe defaults without
    crashing the room, and the stored value is not silently rewritten.

9. PREFERENCE STORAGE AND EXACT KEY INVENTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One new key is introduced by this pass:

  wrong-script.audio-settings.v1
    { v, choice, master, music, effects, ambience, video }
    choice    "" | "sound" | "silent"   (first-visit answer)
    master    boolean                   (overrides all channels)
    music     0..100
    effects   0..100
    ambience  0..100
    video     boolean                   (background video on/off)

Values are validated on read; anything out of range, of the wrong type, or not
JSON at all is replaced in memory by the default and left on disk until the
visitor changes it deliberately.

12. DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED IN THIS PASS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DEF-A  The first-visit sound question covered the page and took clicks
       Found by: node tools/verify-all.js at revision 19, where gallery-ui,
       social-room-ui and creator-studio-ui failed. Playwright reported the
       mixer panel "intercepts pointer events" for the Gallery's category
       filters and for header links.
       Cause: on a first visit the controller called expand(), opening a panel
       over the page before the visitor had answered.
       Why it mattered: it broke real controls, and it contradicted this brief's
       rule that the controller must not obstruct navigation or content.
       Fix: the question now renders as a small strip inside the pill's own
       footprint (buildAsk() in assets/js/media-controller.js, styled as
       .mixer__ask--pill). The panel does not open on a first visit; the page
       keeps every click. The footer reserves 196px (212px on small screens)
       while the question is unanswered.
       Verified: the Gallery suite passes; the refinement suite asserts the
       panel stays closed, the question is inside the pill, and a page filter
       still receives its click.
