The Gallery

A room for looking

Frames, drafts and small things made in this universe: art, constructed stills, poems, prose, line work and one written piece of music. Nothing here is for sale, nothing is ranked, and nothing is uploaded by anybody. It is a room with work in it.

Open now · eight demonstration exhibits · no account needed

What this is, and what it is not

Every exhibit in this room was written or drawn for THE WRONG SCRIPT and is credited to The Wrong Script Studio. There are no member submissions, no public profiles, no comments, no likes and no visitor numbers, because none of those systems exist yet. When member work arrives it will be reviewed before it is shown; there is no upload or publishing control in this Gallery, and no way to add anything to it from the browser.

The Original Music shelf holds a written cue rather than a recording: the project has no original recording of it, so the exhibit shows the score sketch and the intention. Nothing in this Gallery plays sound, and nothing autoplays.

Elsewhere in the building

The Gallery sits alongside the other rooms. The Original Production is the film this universe started as, The Stories holds interactive fiction, The Arcade holds small games, and The Social Room holds curated notices, questions and invitations. The Archive is now open: a read-only catalogue of completed work, collections and discoveries. The Creator Studio is now open as a read-only desk: the formats this universe is built from, the standard a piece has to meet, and the review route a contribution would take. No accounts and no uploads yet, and it says so.
