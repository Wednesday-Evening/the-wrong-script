READ THIS FIRST - WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every asset below was supplied by the project owner, who uploaded the six files
to the repository root in commit ee977d0 ("Add files via upload"). The owner
authorised their integration into five rooms in Phase 10.

That is the entire rights basis this project has. It is an owner's statement,
not a verified licence. Nothing in this file should be read as confirmation that
any asset is cleared for publication.

  Established:      the files exist, are intact, and are playable; the owner
                    supplied them and authorised this integration.

  NOT established:  who made each asset, where it was obtained, under what
                    licence it is distributed, whether attribution is required,
                    whether commercial or public use is permitted.

Contributor names are NOT recorded here as fact. The three audio files carry
studio-style names inside their original filenames (sigmamusicart,
audiovisuaiseu, universfield), which is consistent with the naming used by some
stock-audio libraries, but that is an inference from a filename and this project
will not state it as a credit. No contributor name has been invented or guessed
into the records below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OWNER ACTION REQUIRED BEFORE ANY PUBLIC OR PRODUCTION LAUNCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For each of the six assets, confirm and record:

  1. The original source page (URL or library listing).
  2. The creator or contributor, if the licence requires a named credit.
  3. The exact licence name and version, and whether it permits this use.
  4. Whether attribution must be displayed to visitors, and in what form.
  5. Whether the asset may be used commercially, if that is ever intended.

Until that is done, treat all six as unverified for public release. They are
integrated in this branch on the owner's instruction and must not be described
anywhere as "licensed", "royalty-free" or "cleared".

If any asset turns out to be unusable, remove its reference from the page that
uses it (section "INTENDED PLACEMENT" below) and leave the file in place. The
rooms are written so that removing a media reference degrades to a working page:
the video blocks are poster-first and user-started, and every audio element is
optional and off by default. No room depends on any asset to function.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TEEN-AUDIENCE SUITABILITY - HONEST STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The authorisation requires content suitable for a broad teen audience. What was
actually done:

  - File metadata was inspected for all six assets.
  - One frame was sampled from each video to produce its poster and was viewed.
  - The three audio files were identified by container, codec and duration.

What was NOT done: a full viewing of all three videos, a full listening pass of
the two long audio files, or a frame-by-frame review of any clip. No content
concern was identified in what was inspected, and none of the material is
violent, sexual, hateful or otherwise obviously unsuitable. This is a sampling
result, not a certification, and the owner is the final judge of suitability for
the audience they intend.
