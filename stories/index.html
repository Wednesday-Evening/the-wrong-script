On the shelf

One story on the shelf

This shelf is deliberately short. Everything listed here can be opened and finished tonight; nothing is a placeholder, and no rating, review or reader count is invented to make the room look busier than it is. More stories will be added as they are written, not as promises.

How a story works here

Choices change what you find. Different routes through a story give you different information, and the ending you reach depends on what you know when you get there.

Progress stays in your browser. Each story saves under its own key. Nothing is sent anywhere, there is no account, and the original film’s save is a separate record that these stories never read or touch.

Endings are kept, not scored. A story remembers which endings you have found in this browser so a replay has somewhere to go. That record is yours alone; there is no leaderboard and nobody else can see it.

The Wrong Script: Original Production is not on this shelf. It has its own room and its own save, and it is reached from the Lobby or directly at the original production.
