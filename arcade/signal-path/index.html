How to play

  • Move one cell at a time with the arrow keys, W A S D, or the pad.
  • Hatched dark cells are walls. So is the edge of the grid.
  • Walk over every ◆ fragment before you step on the beacon.
  • A sealed beacon tells you how many fragments are still out there, and the move still counts.
  • Only real moves count. A blocked move leaves the counter alone.
  • No timer, no lives, no score. Restart or undo whenever you like.
