How to play

  • The lamp stays put. It sends one beam in the direction it is pointing.
  • Each mirror has two positions. Turning one changes which way the beam leaves it.
  • Click or tap a mirror, or tab to it and press Enter or Space, to turn it.
  • The beam crosses windows and lights them. It stops on a wall or at the edge of the room.
  • A room is finished when every window in it has been lit at least once.
  • No timer, no moves limit, no score. Reset the mirrors whenever you like.
