The cabinet

Two games in the cabinet

Only finished games are listed here. There is no placeholder card, no invented player count and no score to chase. When another game is ready it will be added to this cabinet and nowhere else.

How this room works

  • No accounts. Nothing here asks who you are, and there is nothing to sign up for. The games are single-player and stay that way.
  • Progress stays in this browser. Each game keeps its own save key, so a game cannot overwrite the film's saved run or a story's place. Clearing site data clears it.
  • No leaderboards, streaks or scores. There is nothing to keep up with and nothing that punishes you for leaving.
  • Nothing is timed. Every game can be played at whatever pace suits you, and every level can be restarted or undone.
  • Sound is off unless a game says otherwise, and no game starts playing audio on its own.
