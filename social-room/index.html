The Social Room

A room with the lights on

The parts of this universe that are better shared: notices from the studio, questions worth carrying around, invitations to try something, and a short listing of what is happening in the building. Four boards, one corridor, no feed to keep up with.

Open now · four boards · curated and read-only · no account needed

Read-only, on purpose

Everything in this room is written by The Wrong Script Studio. There are no member accounts, no profiles, no comments, no reactions and no attendance figures, because none of those systems exist yet. Nothing here is submitted, counted or stored, and the room keeps no save of its own.

Community features are not available in this first version, and no date is being promised for them. The four boards are here to be read, and every link on them leads to a room that actually exists.
